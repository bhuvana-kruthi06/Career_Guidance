# Phase 5 — Server-side persistence

## 1. Summary (5 lines)

Added Flyway V2 tables for profiles, match sessions/results, saved careers, and roadmap items.  
Authenticated matches now store the verified owner ID, skill/interests snapshot, results, and timestamp.  
Profile, session history/detail/delete, saved-career, roadmap, import-local, and delete-account endpoints are owner-scoped.  
The account page now shows match history, saved session details, and a progress chart sourced from saved sessions.  
Guests retain browser-local behavior; database persistence and imports were verified with H2 MySQL mode.
